# Project approval reference summary

Source reviewed: supplied `When_Nature_Strikes.pptx` approval presentation.

- **Title:** When Nature Strikes: A Visualization Approach to Determine the Impact of Natural Disasters.
- **Research question:** Are the most frequent disasters also the most destructive?
- **Dataset:** EM-DAT, the International Disaster Database.
- **Proposed scope:** Historical natural disasters, including earthquakes, floods, cyclones/storms, wildfires, droughts, and other natural hazard types.
- **Dimensions named in the deck:** Disaster type, location, magnitude/severity, deaths and affected population, and economic damage.
- **Visualization direction:** Exploratory analysis and comparison of disaster frequency against destructiveness, with an interactive dashboard.
- **Unit coverage written in the deck:** Unit 1 and Unit 3.
