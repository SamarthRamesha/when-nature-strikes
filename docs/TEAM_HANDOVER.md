# Team handover

Use the [submission index](../submission/README.md) to review the report,
slides and demo. The [validation report](VALIDATION_REPORT.md) records what
ran. The [contribution matrix](TEAM_CONTRIBUTIONS.md) records the distinction
between pre-existing work and Codex assistance.

- Review statistical interpretation, report and presenter material together.
- Keep the authorized workbook in local storage and verify its fingerprint
  against [provenance.json](../results/provenance.json).
- If a native PBIX is required by the evaluator, use the [Power BI package](../dashboard/powerbi/README.md)
  on Windows. Check filter interactions and the map, then obtain internal approval.
- Confirm the contribution record, required institutional declarations and data
  provider guidance on historical public exposure before submission.
- The branch targets main for review. Any merge needs the repository owner's approval.
